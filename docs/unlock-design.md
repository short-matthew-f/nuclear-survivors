# Unlock progression research

Reviewed October 13, 2026. This is a design comparison, not a claim that Nuclear Survivors duplicates another game's economy.

## Comparisons

- **Vampire Survivors:** its Unlocks achievements reward characters, weapons, stages and features. Surviving with characters and upgrading weapons creates further goals. Source: [community Unlocks reference](https://vampire-survivors.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements). The official [Adventures FAQ](https://poncle.games/adventures-faq) also describes weapon purchases that expand future level-up choices within an Adventure; that behavior is specific to Adventures.
- **Brotato:** characters are earned through distinct feats, including speed, healing, structures, and survival. Winning with individual characters unlocks weapons or items. This encourages experimentation with different builds. Source: [community Progress reference](https://brotato.wiki.spellsandguns.com/Progress). Its in-operation shop is separate from these persistent achievement unlocks; we should not confuse shop materials with our permanent scrap balance.
- **Soulstone Survivors:** its official site presents character unlocking, weapon crafting, secret spells, and a skill tree as complementary progression systems. Source: [official game site](https://soulstonesurvivors.com/). That supports offering different progression choices, but does not establish specific prices or unlock conditions.

## Application to Nuclear Survivors

Our previous system was mostly a price list. The inference from these comparisons is that visible, themed goals can make the next operation more purposeful. Currency alone gives less reason to change playstyle.

Use free field feats alongside direct scrap purchases. Do not add a challenge _and_ a purchase requirement, remove earned access, copy large numerical thresholds from longer games, or introduce random recruitment. Retain scrap as a choice between obtaining a new playstyle sooner and buying permanent research.

Early goals (two-minute survival, 100 infected, first titan, first rescue) teach core actions. Specialization and full-arsenal goals encourage different builds. Cumulative enemy hunts create later goals that still advance on unsuccessful operations. Hero rewards bundle their signature gear, so a new recruit immediately offers a complete starting identity. Ten concise goals fit the current roster; they are not a promise of an endless progression tree.

Progress is shown in the Bunker, credited at operation settlement, and included in backups. Existing historical evidence grants matching rewards on migration without spending scrap. A hero already purchased does not produce duplicate rewards or repeat charges. No previously owned weapons or pending run choices are removed.

The exact two-minute/100-kill/20-specialist/50-Stray targets are our initial tuning for eight-minute operations, not values copied from other games. Playtest pacing before adding more tiers or currencies. Future character mastery rewards should add distinct gear or build options rather than only escalating permanent damage.
